# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Retail Forex and Crypto traders practicing and reviewing trades. Two modes of use: logging real trades for structured AI feedback (journal), and rehearsing strategies against historical price action without risking capital (backtest + bar-by-bar replay). They sit down for focused sessions — replay practice is a flow-state activity with hotkeys, chart interaction, and split-second decisions. They read charts fluently, think in pips, R:R, risk-%. Mobile use is real but secondary: checking reviews, journaling on the go.

## Product Purpose

TradeAI is an AI-powered trade journal and backtesting platform. It turns raw trading activity into disciplined improvement: five AI reviewer personalities score every trade or backtest from distinct professional lenses (prop-firm evaluator, psychologist, institutional trader, quant, risk manager). The replay platform simulates live trading on historical OHLCV so traders can build execution habits safely. Success looks like: a trader who journals consistently, can articulate their recurring mistakes (the reviewers name them), and whose replay behavior shows growing discipline — SL always set, risk sized correctly, no revenge entries.

## Brand Personality

Coach-supportive within a terminal-precise frame. Three words: **honest, disciplined, encouraging**. The interface speaks the trader's language fluently (R:R, BE, ATR, trail) but never lets density become coldness — the AI coach and review voices are candid like a good mentor, not brutal like a leaderboard. Numbers are sacred: monospace, exact, never rounded deceptively. Feedback at high-stakes moments (placing a trade, ending a session) is calm and reassuring, because the tool is training habits that must transfer to live markets.

## Anti-references

- **Robinhood-gamify**: confetti, dopamine loops, streak-baiting notifications, celebratory animation on every fill. Trading is practice, not a slot machine.
- **Legacy-terminal clutter**: gray walls of numbers, zero hierarchy, everything shouting. Density must be organized — grouped, labeled, prioritized — not merely abundant.
- Also avoid generic AI-SaaS clichés: purple gradients, glassy cards, vague "magic" language that hides how scoring actually works. The AI's verdicts should feel auditable, not oracle-like.

## Design Principles

1. **Discipline by design** — the UI makes the right trading habit the easy path (SL set before entry, risk visible before confirm) and makes destructive actions deliberate, never accidental.
2. **Numbers are sacred** — exact monospace figures, honest precision per instrument, green/red reserved strictly for gain/loss meaning.
3. **Calm at the peaks** — moments of commitment (order placement, session end) get the clearest feedback and most reassurance; friction belongs on irreversible actions, not routine ones.
4. **Dense but organized** — terminal information richness with clear grouping and hierarchy; every element earns its pixel but nothing important hides behind hover-only discovery.
5. **Coach in the corner, not the cockpit** — AI guidance supports the trader's own decision-making; it advises at the moment of need without hijacking the trading surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus rings, keyboard paths for all primary flows, `aria-live` for trade events (fills, SL hits, margin calls), no color-only state meaning, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation. Canvas-drawn layers (drawings, SL/TP overlays) need textual equivalents or accessible fallback controls.
