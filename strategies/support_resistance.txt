====================================================
SUPPORT & RESISTANCE FOREX TRADING STRATEGY
Version: 1.0
Style: Support & Resistance
Market: Forex

Recommended Currency Pairs:
- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- AUD/USD
- NZD/USD
- USD/CAD
- EUR/JPY
- GBP/JPY

Timeframes:
Trend: 4H
Setup: 1H
Entry: 15M

====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Trade reversals from strong support and resistance levels
using candlestick confirmation.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Indicators:

- 200 EMA (Trend Filter)
- ATR (14)

No oscillators required.

====================================================
STEP 1: IDENTIFY TREND
====================================================

Bullish:

- Price above 200 EMA

Bearish:

- Price below 200 EMA

Trade only in the direction of the trend.

====================================================
STEP 2: DRAW LEVELS
====================================================

Mark:

- Previous Daily High
- Previous Daily Low
- Weekly High
- Weekly Low
- Swing Highs
- Swing Lows

A level becomes valid after at least
2 touches without breaking.

====================================================
STEP 3: WAIT FOR PRICE
====================================================

Do not enter immediately.

Wait until price reaches a marked level.

====================================================
LONG ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Uptrend.
2. Price touches support.
3. Bullish rejection candle forms.

Valid candles:

- Hammer
- Bullish Engulfing
- Bullish Pin Bar

Entry:

Buy at candle close.

====================================================
SHORT ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Downtrend.
2. Price touches resistance.
3. Bearish rejection candle forms.

Valid candles:

- Shooting Star
- Bearish Engulfing
- Bearish Pin Bar

Entry:

Sell at candle close.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long:

Below support or swing low.

Short:

Above resistance or swing high.

Minimum distance:

1.5 × ATR

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Primary:

Risk Reward = 1:2

Alternative:

Next support or resistance level.

====================================================
TRADE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

+1R

Move stop to break even.

+2R

Close 50%.

Trail remaining position behind
new swing highs/lows.

====================================================
FILTERS
====================================================

Avoid:

- Major news
- Sideways markets
- Weak levels
- Less than two touches

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT
====================================================

Risk:

1% per trade

Maximum:

2%

Daily loss:

3%

Weekly loss:

6%

Stop after:

3 losing trades.

====================================================
RULES SUMMARY
====================================================

✓ Trade with trend.

✓ Use confirmed support/resistance.

✓ Wait for rejection candle.

✓ Risk 1%.

✓ Minimum 2:1 Reward/Risk.

✓ Never chase price.

====================================================
