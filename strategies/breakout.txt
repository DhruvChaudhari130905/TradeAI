====================================================
BREAKOUT FOREX TRADING STRATEGY
Version: 1.0
Style: Breakout Trading
Market: Forex

Recommended Currency Pairs:
- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- AUD/USD
- NZD/USD
- USD/CAD
- EUR/JPY
- GBP/JPY

Timeframes:
Trend: 4H
Setup: 1H
Entry: 15M

====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Identify periods of consolidation and enter trades when price
breaks out with strong momentum.

The goal is to capture the beginning of a new trend.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Indicators (Optional):

- 20 EMA
- ATR (14)
- Volume (if available)

No oscillators are required.

====================================================
STEP 1: IDENTIFY CONSOLIDATION
====================================================

Look for a tight trading range where:

- Price moves sideways.
- At least 8 consecutive candles remain within the range.
- Highs and lows are relatively equal.
- Volatility decreases.

Mark:

- Highest High of the range.
- Lowest Low of the range.

This forms the breakout zone.

====================================================
STEP 2: CONFIRM MARKET CONDITIONS
====================================================

Trade only when:

- No major economic news is scheduled within the next 30 minutes.
- Market is not extremely volatile due to unexpected events.
- The breakout occurs during active trading sessions.

Preferred sessions:

- London Session
- New York Session
- London/New York overlap

====================================================
STEP 3: LONG ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Price closes above the consolidation high.
2. Breakout candle closes outside the range.
3. Candle body is larger than the average of the previous 5 candles.
4. Breakout is not immediately rejected.

Entry:

Buy at the close of the breakout candle.

Alternative:

Place a Buy Stop 2 pips above the breakout candle high.

====================================================
STEP 4: SHORT ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Price closes below the consolidation low.
2. Candle closes outside the range.
3. Strong bearish breakout candle.
4. No immediate rejection.

Entry:

Sell at the close of the breakout candle.

Alternative:

Place a Sell Stop 2 pips below the breakout candle low.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long Trade

Place stop loss:

- Below the breakout candle low

OR

- Below the consolidation range

Use whichever is further.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Trade

Place stop loss:

- Above breakout candle high

OR

- Above consolidation range

Use whichever is further.

====================================================
POSITION SIZE
====================================================

Risk per trade:

1%

Maximum:

2%

Adjust position size based on stop-loss distance.

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Method 1

Risk Reward:

1 : 2

Example:

Risk = 25 pips

Target = 50 pips

----------------------------------------------------

Method 2

Measure the height of the consolidation range.

Project that distance from the breakout point.

Example:

Range Height = 40 pips

Breakout occurs at 1.1000

Target = 1.1040

====================================================
TRADE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

At +1R:

Move stop loss to break even.

At +2R:

Optionally close 50% of the position.

Trail the remaining position below higher lows
(for long trades) or above lower highs
(for short trades).

====================================================
FALSE BREAKOUT FILTER
====================================================

Avoid entering if:

- Candle closes back inside the range.
- Long upper or lower wick shows rejection.
- Breakout occurs with a very small candle.
- Price immediately reverses after breaking out.

Wait for a clean close outside the range.

====================================================
OPTIONAL RETEST ENTRY
====================================================

Instead of entering immediately:

Wait for price to return and retest the breakout level.

Enter only if:

- The level holds as support (long)
OR
- The level holds as resistance (short)

This reduces false breakouts.

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT
====================================================

Maximum daily loss:

3%

Maximum weekly loss:

6%

Stop trading after:

3 consecutive losing trades.

Never increase risk to recover losses.

====================================================
VALID BREAKOUT PATTERNS
====================================================

Trade:

- Rectangle Breakout
- Horizontal Range Breakout
- Bull Flag Breakout
- Bear Flag Breakout
- Ascending Triangle Breakout
- Descending Triangle Breakout

Avoid:

- Random spikes
- Low-liquidity breakouts
- Breakouts during major news releases

====================================================
LONG TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

EUR/USD

Price consolidates between:

1.1050

and

1.1080

After 10 candles,
a strong bullish candle closes above 1.1080.

Entry:

Buy at close.

Stop:

Below consolidation low.

Target:

Height of range projected upward
or 2R.

====================================================
SHORT TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

GBP/USD

Price consolidates for several hours.

Strong bearish candle closes below support.

Entry:

Sell at close.

Stop:

Above consolidation high.

Target:

Twice the risk
or projected range height.

====================================================
RULES SUMMARY
====================================================

✓ Trade only confirmed breakouts.

✓ Wait for candle close outside the range.

✓ Risk only 1% per trade.

✓ Use a protective stop loss.

✓ Target at least 2:1 Reward/Risk.

✓ Avoid trading during major news.

✓ Prefer London and New York sessions.

✓ Ignore weak or false breakouts.

✓ Journal every trade.
