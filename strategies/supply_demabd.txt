====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Identify institutional buying and selling zones
where price is likely to reverse.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Indicators:

- 200 EMA
- ATR (14)

No oscillators required.

====================================================
STEP 1: IDENTIFY TREND
====================================================

Bullish:

Price above 200 EMA.

Bearish:

Price below 200 EMA.

Trade only with trend.

====================================================
STEP 2: IDENTIFY ZONES
====================================================

Demand Zone

A strong bullish move starts from
a small consolidation.

Pattern:

Base → Rally

Supply Zone

A strong bearish move starts from
a small consolidation.

Pattern:

Base → Drop

Requirements:

- At least 3 strong candles leave the base.
- Large candle bodies.
- Small consolidation area.

====================================================
STEP 3: WAIT FOR RETEST
====================================================

Never enter during the initial move.

Wait until price revisits
the supply or demand zone.

====================================================
LONG ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Uptrend.
2. Price reaches demand zone.
3. Bullish rejection candle forms.

Accepted candles:

- Hammer
- Bullish Engulfing
- Bullish Pin Bar

Entry:

Buy at candle close.

====================================================
SHORT ENTRY
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Downtrend.
2. Price reaches supply zone.
3. Bearish rejection candle forms.

Accepted candles:

- Shooting Star
- Bearish Engulfing
- Bearish Pin Bar

Entry:

Sell at candle close.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long:

Below demand zone.

Short:

Above supply zone.

Add:

5 pip buffer

or

1 × ATR

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Method 1

2R

Method 2

Next opposing zone.

====================================================
TRADE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

+1R

Move stop to break even.

+2R

Close half.

Allow remaining trade
to reach next zone.

====================================================
VALID ZONES
====================================================

Trade only fresh zones.

A fresh zone has:

- Never been revisited.
- Strong departure.
- Clear imbalance.

Avoid:

- Multiple retests.
- Weak departures.
- Choppy bases.

====================================================
FILTERS
====================================================

Do not trade:

- News events.
- Weak zones.
- Sideways markets.
- Counter-trend setups.
