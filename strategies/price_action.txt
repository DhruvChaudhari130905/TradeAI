====================================================
PRICE ACTION FOREX TRADING STRATEGY
Version: 1.0
Style: Trend Following with Price Action
Market: Forex
Recommended Pairs:
- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- AUD/USD
- USD/CAD
- NZD/USD
- EUR/JPY
- GBP/JPY

Timeframes:
Trend: 4H
Setup: 1H
Entry: 15M

====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Trade only in the direction of the overall trend using naked price action.
No oscillators or lagging indicators are required.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Charts only.

Optional:
- 200 EMA (trend filter only)

No RSI
No MACD
No Bollinger Bands

====================================================
STEP 1: DETERMINE TREND
====================================================

Bullish Trend
--------------
- Higher Highs
- Higher Lows

Bearish Trend
--------------
- Lower Highs
- Lower Lows

If price is moving sideways for more than 20 candles,
DO NOT TRADE.

====================================================
STEP 2: MARK IMPORTANT LEVELS
====================================================

Draw horizontal lines at:

- Previous Daily High
- Previous Daily Low
- Weekly High
- Weekly Low
- Major Swing Highs
- Major Swing Lows

These become support and resistance zones.

====================================================
STEP 3: WAIT FOR A PULLBACK
====================================================

Never chase price.

Wait until price pulls back toward:

- Support in an uptrend
OR
- Resistance in a downtrend

The pullback should consist of several candles and show slowing momentum.

====================================================
STEP 4: ENTRY SIGNAL
====================================================

Long Entry

Requirements:

1. Uptrend
2. Pullback into support
3. Bullish rejection candle

Accepted candles:

- Bullish Pin Bar
- Bullish Engulfing
- Strong Bullish Outside Bar

Enter at the close of the confirmation candle
or
Place a Buy Stop 2 pips above its high.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Entry

Requirements:

1. Downtrend
2. Pullback into resistance
3. Bearish rejection candle

Accepted candles:

- Bearish Pin Bar
- Bearish Engulfing
- Strong Bearish Outside Bar

Enter at candle close
or
Sell Stop 2 pips below its low.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long Trade

Place stop loss:

- Below the rejection candle
OR
- Below the recent swing low

Add 3-5 pip buffer.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Trade

Place stop:

- Above rejection candle
OR
- Above recent swing high

Add 3-5 pip buffer.

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Primary target:

Risk Reward = 1:2

Example:

Risk = 30 pips

Target = 60 pips

Alternative exits:

- Next resistance
- Next support
- Previous swing high
- Previous swing low

====================================================
TRADE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

When trade reaches +1R:

Move stop to Break Even.

When trade reaches +1.5R:

Optionally close 50%.

Allow remaining position to reach target.

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT
====================================================

Risk per trade:

1%

Maximum:

2%

Maximum daily loss:

3%

Maximum weekly loss:

6%

Stop trading after:

3 consecutive losing trades.

====================================================
FILTERS
====================================================

Do NOT trade:

- During major economic news
- During low liquidity
- During holidays
- During ranging markets

Trade only during:

London Session

or

London + New York overlap.

====================================================
VALID PATTERNS
====================================================

Use only:

- Pin Bar
- Bullish Engulfing
- Bearish Engulfing
- Inside Bar Breakout
- Outside Bar
- Break and Retest
- Trend Continuation

Avoid:

- Doji
- Tiny candles
- Choppy consolidation

====================================================
EXAMPLE LONG TRADE
====================================================

EUR/USD

Trend:
Higher highs and higher lows.

Price retraces to previous support.

Bullish pin bar forms.

Entry:
Buy above pin bar.

Stop:
Below pin bar.

Target:
2 × stop distance.

====================================================
EXAMPLE SHORT TRADE
====================================================

GBP/USD

Trend:
Lower highs and lower lows.

Price retraces to resistance.

Bearish engulfing candle forms.

Entry:
Sell below engulfing candle.

Stop:
Above candle.

Target:
2 × stop distance.

====================================================
RULES SUMMARY
====================================================

✓ Trade only with trend.

✓ Wait for pullback.

✓ Enter only after confirmation candle.

✓ Always use stop loss.

✓ Minimum 1:2 Risk Reward.

✓ Risk only 1% per trade.

✓ Avoid news events.

✓ Never revenge trade.

✓ Do not move stop loss further away.

✓ Journal every trade.
