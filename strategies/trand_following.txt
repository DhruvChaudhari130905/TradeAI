====================================================
TREND FOLLOWING FOREX TRADING STRATEGY
Version: 1.0
Style: Trend Following
Market: Forex

Recommended Currency Pairs:
- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- AUD/USD
- NZD/USD
- USD/CAD
- EUR/JPY
- GBP/JPY

Timeframes:
Trend: Daily
Setup: 4H
Entry: 1H

====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Capture large market trends by trading only in the direction
of the prevailing trend.

This strategy focuses on staying in winning trades as long
as possible while cutting losing trades quickly.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Indicators:

- 50 EMA
- 200 EMA
- ATR (14)

====================================================
STEP 1: DETERMINE THE TREND
====================================================

Bullish Trend

Conditions:

- Price is above the 200 EMA.
- 50 EMA is above the 200 EMA.
- Both EMAs are sloping upward.

----------------------------------------------------

Bearish Trend

Conditions:

- Price is below the 200 EMA.
- 50 EMA is below the 200 EMA.
- Both EMAs are sloping downward.

----------------------------------------------------

No Trade

Do not trade if:

- EMAs are crossing frequently.
- Price is moving sideways.
- EMAs are flat.

====================================================
STEP 2: WAIT FOR A PULLBACK
====================================================

Never enter immediately after a strong move.

Wait until price retraces toward:

- 50 EMA
OR
- Recent swing level

The pullback should be controlled and consist of
small candles rather than aggressive reversals.

====================================================
STEP 3: ENTRY CONDITIONS
====================================================

LONG TRADE

Requirements:

1. Bullish trend confirmed.
2. Price pulls back to the 50 EMA.
3. A bullish confirmation candle forms.

Accepted confirmation candles:

- Bullish Engulfing
- Bullish Pin Bar
- Strong Bullish Candle

Enter at the close of the confirmation candle.

----------------------------------------------------

SHORT TRADE

Requirements:

1. Bearish trend confirmed.
2. Price pulls back to the 50 EMA.
3. A bearish confirmation candle forms.

Accepted confirmation candles:

- Bearish Engulfing
- Bearish Pin Bar
- Strong Bearish Candle

Enter at the close of the confirmation candle.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long Trade

Place stop loss:

- Below recent swing low

OR

- 1.5 × ATR below entry

Use whichever distance is larger.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Trade

Place stop loss:

- Above recent swing high

OR

- 1.5 × ATR above entry

Use whichever distance is larger.

====================================================
POSITION SIZE
====================================================

Risk per trade:

1%

Maximum:

2%

Calculate lot size using stop-loss distance.

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Primary Method

No fixed target.

Hold the trade until trend ends.

Exit when:

- Price closes on the opposite side of the 50 EMA.

OR

- 50 EMA crosses the 200 EMA.

----------------------------------------------------

Alternative Method

Take partial profit at:

2R

Move stop loss to break even.

Allow remaining position to ride the trend.

====================================================
TRAILING STOP
====================================================

Option 1

Trail below each new swing low.

----------------------------------------------------

Option 2

Trail using:

2 × ATR

====================================================
EXIT CONDITIONS
====================================================

Exit immediately if:

- Trend reverses.
- Opposite EMA crossover occurs.
- Strong reversal candle appears after extended trend.
- Major economic event causes abnormal volatility.

====================================================
FILTERS
====================================================

Avoid trading:

- During major economic news.
- During holidays.
- During extremely low volatility.
- During narrow ranging markets.

Trade during:

- London Session.
- New York Session.
- London/New York overlap.

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT
====================================================

Maximum daily loss:

3%

Maximum weekly loss:

6%

Stop trading after:

3 consecutive losing trades.

Never increase risk after a loss.

====================================================
VALID SETUPS
====================================================

Only trade:

- EMA pullbacks
- Trend continuation
- Break and retest
- Bull flags
- Bear flags
- Strong impulse followed by controlled correction

Avoid:

- Choppy markets
- Sideways ranges
- Counter-trend trades

====================================================
LONG TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

EUR/USD

Trend:

Price above both EMAs.

50 EMA above 200 EMA.

Price pulls back to the 50 EMA.

Bullish engulfing candle forms.

Entry:

Buy at candle close.

Stop:

Below recent swing low.

Exit:

Trail stop until price closes below the 50 EMA.

====================================================
SHORT TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

GBP/USD

Trend:

Price below both EMAs.

50 EMA below 200 EMA.

Price retraces to the 50 EMA.

Bearish pin bar forms.

Entry:

Sell at candle close.

Stop:

Above recent swing high.

Exit:

Trail stop until price closes above the 50 EMA.

====================================================
RULES SUMMARY
====================================================

✓ Trade only with the primary trend.

✓ Wait for pullbacks.

✓ Never chase price.

✓ Confirm entry with price action.

✓ Risk only 1% per trade.

✓ Use ATR-based stop loss.

✓ Let winners run.

✓ Cut losers quickly.

✓ Never average down.

✓ Journal every trade.
