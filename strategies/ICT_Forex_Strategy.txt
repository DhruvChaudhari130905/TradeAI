ICT FOREX TRADING STRATEGY (PLAIN ENGLISH)

DISCLAIMER

This strategy is for educational and backtesting purposes only. It does
not guarantee profits.

MARKET

-   Pair: EUR/USD (can also be tested on GBP/USD)
-   Timeframe: 5 Minute
-   Higher Timeframe: 15 Minute

TOOLS

1.  20 EMA
2.  Previous Day High
3.  Previous Day Low
4.  Session High and Low (London and New York)
5.  Fair Value Gap (FVG)
6.  Market Structure (Higher Highs, Higher Lows, Lower Highs, Lower
    Lows)

RULES

STEP 1 – DETERMINE THE TREND - Open the 15-minute chart. - If price is
making higher highs and higher lows, only look for BUY trades. - If
price is making lower highs and lower lows, only look for SELL trades. -
Ignore trades against the trend.

STEP 2 – WAIT FOR A LIQUIDITY SWEEP For a BUY: - Wait until price moves
below a recent swing low or the previous day’s low. - Price should
quickly move back above that level.

For a SELL: - Wait until price moves above a recent swing high or the
previous day’s high. - Price should quickly move back below that level.

STEP 3 – WAIT FOR MARKET STRUCTURE SHIFT BUY: - After sweeping a low,
wait until price breaks a recent lower high.

SELL: - After sweeping a high, wait until price breaks a recent higher
low.

Do not enter before this confirmation.

STEP 4 – FIND A FAIR VALUE GAP BUY: - Look for a bullish Fair Value Gap
after the structure shift.

SELL: - Look for a bearish Fair Value Gap after the structure shift.

STEP 5 – ENTRY BUY: - Place a Buy Limit at the Fair Value Gap. OR -
Enter at market if price immediately rejects from the gap.

SELL: - Place a Sell Limit at the Fair Value Gap. OR - Enter at market
if price rejects from the gap.

STOP LOSS

BUY: - Place the stop below the liquidity sweep low.

SELL: - Place the stop above the liquidity sweep high.

TAKE PROFIT

Target one of these: - Next swing high/low - Previous day’s high or
low - Risk to Reward of at least 1:2

RISK MANAGEMENT

-   Risk only 1% of account per trade.
-   Maximum 2 trades per day.
-   Stop trading after 2 losses.
-   Never move the stop loss further away.

TRADE CHECKLIST

Before entering: [ ] Trend matches direction. [ ] Liquidity sweep
occurred. [ ] Market structure shifted. [ ] Fair Value Gap formed. [ ]
Risk-to-Reward is at least 1:2. [ ] Risk is within limits.

BACKTEST RULES

-   Test at least 100 trades.
-   Record:
    -   Date
    -   Time
    -   Pair
    -   Entry
    -   Stop Loss
    -   Take Profit
    -   Win or Loss
    -   Risk-to-Reward
    -   Notes

Do not change the rules during the test. Only evaluate performance after
all trades are completed.
