====================================================
MEAN REVERSION FOREX TRADING STRATEGY
Version: 1.0
Style: Mean Reversion
Market: Forex

Recommended Currency Pairs:
- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- AUD/USD
- NZD/USD
- USD/CAD
- EUR/JPY
- GBP/JPY

Timeframes:
Trend Filter: 4H
Setup: 1H
Entry: 15M

====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Identify situations where price has moved significantly
away from its average and trade the expected return
toward the mean.

This strategy works best in ranging or slow-moving markets.

====================================================
TOOLS
====================================================

Indicators:

- 20 EMA
- Bollinger Bands (20, 2)
- RSI (14)
- ATR (14)

====================================================
STEP 1: DETERMINE MARKET CONDITION
====================================================

Trade only when:

- Price is moving sideways.
- 20 EMA is relatively flat.
- No strong trend exists.
- Price repeatedly oscillates around the 20 EMA.

Do NOT trade if:

- Price is making strong higher highs and higher lows.
- Price is making strong lower highs and lower lows.
- Major breakout has recently occurred.

====================================================
STEP 2: LONG SETUP
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Price touches or closes below the lower Bollinger Band.
2. RSI is below 30.
3. Price remains above major long-term support.
4. A bullish reversal candle forms.

Accepted confirmation candles:

- Bullish Engulfing
- Bullish Pin Bar
- Hammer

====================================================
LONG ENTRY
====================================================

Enter Buy:

At the close of the bullish confirmation candle.

====================================================
STEP 3: SHORT SETUP
====================================================

Conditions:

1. Price touches or closes above the upper Bollinger Band.
2. RSI is above 70.
3. Price remains below major long-term resistance.
4. A bearish reversal candle forms.

Accepted confirmation candles:

- Bearish Engulfing
- Shooting Star
- Bearish Pin Bar

====================================================
SHORT ENTRY
====================================================

Enter Sell:

At the close of the bearish confirmation candle.

====================================================
STOP LOSS
====================================================

Long Trade

Place stop loss:

- Below the recent swing low

OR

- 1.5 × ATR below entry

Use whichever is further.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Trade

Place stop loss:

- Above recent swing high

OR

- 1.5 × ATR above entry

Use whichever is further.

====================================================
TAKE PROFIT
====================================================

Primary Target

Exit when price reaches:

20 EMA

----------------------------------------------------

Secondary Target

Exit at the opposite Bollinger Band.

----------------------------------------------------

Alternative

Use a fixed

1 : 2

Risk Reward Ratio.

====================================================
TRADE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

At +1R:

Move stop loss to Break Even.

At +1.5R:

Optionally close 50% of the position.

Allow remaining position to reach:

- 20 EMA
OR
- Opposite Bollinger Band

====================================================
FALSE SIGNAL FILTER
====================================================

Do NOT enter if:

- Candle closes far outside the Bollinger Band with strong momentum.
- High-impact news is due within 30 minutes.
- RSI remains extreme for multiple candles during a strong trend.
- ATR is expanding rapidly after a breakout.

====================================================
OPTIONAL DIVERGENCE FILTER
====================================================

Increase confidence when:

Long Trade:

Price makes a lower low
while RSI makes a higher low.

----------------------------------------------------

Short Trade:

Price makes a higher high
while RSI makes a lower high.

====================================================
RISK MANAGEMENT
====================================================

Risk per trade:

1%

Maximum:

2%

Maximum daily loss:

3%

Maximum weekly loss:

6%

Stop trading after:

3 consecutive losing trades.

====================================================
BEST TRADING SESSIONS
====================================================

Preferred:

- London Session
- Early New York Session

Avoid:

- Late Friday
- Holidays
- Major news releases

====================================================
VALID SETUPS
====================================================

Trade:

- Bollinger Band Rejections
- RSI Overbought/Oversold Reversals
- Support Bounce
- Resistance Rejection
- Double Top
- Double Bottom

Avoid:

- Strong breakouts
- Trending markets
- News-driven volatility

====================================================
LONG TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

EUR/USD

Price touches the lower Bollinger Band.

RSI = 24.

Bullish engulfing candle forms.

Entry:

Buy at candle close.

Stop:

Below swing low.

Target:

20 EMA

or

Upper Bollinger Band.

====================================================
SHORT TRADE EXAMPLE
====================================================

Currency Pair:

GBP/USD

Price reaches the upper Bollinger Band.

RSI = 75.

Bearish shooting star forms.

Entry:

Sell at candle close.

Stop:

Above swing high.

Target:

20 EMA

or

Lower Bollinger Band.

====================================================
RULES SUMMARY
====================================================

✓ Trade only in ranging markets.

✓ Wait for price to reach Bollinger Bands.

✓ Confirm with RSI.

✓ Wait for reversal candlestick.

✓ Risk only 1% per trade.

✓ Use ATR-based stop loss.

✓ Exit near the mean.

✓ Avoid strong trends.

✓ Avoid major news.

✓ Journal every trade.
